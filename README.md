Applied Spatial Ecology: Predicting Invasive Plant Establishment Risk of Pyrus calleryana (2025-2050)

This project focuses on predicting the establishment risk and potential expansion of the invasive Callery Pear (*Pyrus calleryana*) across the Mid-Atlantic region (centered on Virginia and surrounding watersheds) under present-day (2025) baseline and mid-century (2050) climate conditions. I am conducting this analysis to identify geographic areas most vulnerable to invasive plant encroachment, helping regional resource managers prioritize early detection and mitigation efforts before habitat degradation occurs. The final takeaway output will be a high-resolution predictive risk map comparing present-day suitability against future spatial expansion patterns across the study area.
